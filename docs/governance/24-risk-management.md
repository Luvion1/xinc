# 25. Risk Management

## Risk Categories

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Schedule delay | Medium | High | Buffer, prioritize |
| Technical debt | High | Medium | Refactor sprints |
| Security vulnerability | Low | Critical | Security audit |
| Dependency vulnerability | Medium | High | Dependency management |
| Key person loss | Low | High | Knowledge sharing |

## Issue Severity

| Severity | Definition | Response Time |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| Critical | System unusable / data loss | 1 hour |
| High | Major feature broken | 24 hours |
| Medium | Feature impaired | 1 week |
| Low | Minor issue | Next release |
